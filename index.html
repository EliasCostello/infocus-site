How it works

Media stays covered

Photos and videos are blurred, frosted or blacked out. The rest of the post is untouched, so you still see who posted and what they said.

Hold to reveal

Press and hold anything you actually want to see. You can make the wait grow a little with each reveal, so mindless tapping gets slower.

No endless scroll

Turn off Reels and Shorts swiping, hide their buttons and feed sections, and hide comment sections if you want to.

Lock your choices

Lock a setting on and it stays on. Unlocking means solving a math problem first, a moment to decide if you really want to.

Stays on your phone

Your settings live on your device. InFocus has no account and sends nothing to us. Read the privacy policy.
